Privacy Policy

Poker Trainer — 6-Max NLHE · Last updated: 2026-10-29

1. Summary

Poker Trainer is an offline poker training app. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you do stays on your own device.

2. What we store (on your device only)

  • Game settings (blinds, theme, language)
  • Hand history and saved game state
  • Achievement progress and statistics

This data is kept in your browser's localStorage (or the app's private storage on mobile). It never leaves your device, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. What we do NOT do

  • No account, sign-up, email or phone number required
  • No analytics, tracking SDKs or advertising identifiers
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera
  • No real-money gambling, payments, or in-app purchases of chips
  • No data sold or shared with third parties

4. Network access

Gameplay never sends your data anywhere: no account, no sync, no upload of hands or statistics.

The Windows and web versions are delivered as a hosted Progressive Web App. On first launch the app downloads its own files (HTML, JavaScript, styles, language packs) from our static hosting (GitHub Pages). After that the files are cached on your device and the app runs offline. As with any website, the hosting provider may record standard server logs such as IP address and user agent while serving those files; we do not receive, store or analyse those logs.

The mobile (Android) version bundles the same files inside the app and needs no network at all.

If you use the optional "save hand to computer" feature of the web version, review files are written only to a folder you choose on your own machine.

5. Children

The app contains simulated poker play with virtual chips and no real money. It is intended for users aged 13+ (or the minimum age required in your region for simulated gambling content). We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Purchases (store versions)

If you buy the app from an app store, the store operator (Google or Microsoft) processes the payment. We receive only aggregated sales reports, never your payment details or identity.

7. Your control

You can erase all stored data at any time by using "Reset" inside the app, or by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: raoliu.rl@gmail.com

隐私政策（中文）

概述

本应用是以离线为主的德州扑克训练工具，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据，手牌、成就与设置只保存在你自己的设备上。

本地保存的内容

  • 游戏设置（盲注、主题、语言）
  • 手牌历史与存档
  • 成就进度与统计

这些数据存放在浏览器的 localStorage（手机端为应用私有目录），不会离开设备，卸载应用或清除数据即被删除。

我们不做的事

  • 不需要注册账号、邮箱或手机号
  • 不接入统计、追踪 SDK 或广告标识
  • 不访问通讯录、相册、位置、麦克风、摄像头
  • 不涉及真钱赌博、充值或筹码内购
  • 不向第三方出售或共享数据

网络访问

游戏过程不会向任何地方发送你的数据：不需要账号，不同步，不上传手牌与统计。

Windows 版与网页版以「托管型 PWA」方式提供：首次启动时从我们的静态托管（GitHub Pages）下载应用自身的文件（HTML、JavaScript、样式、语言包），之后缓存在你的设备上，可离线运行。与任何网站一样，托管方在提供这些文件时可能记录标准服务器日志（如 IP 地址、浏览器标识），我们不会接收、保存或分析这些日志。

手机（Android）版把同样的文件打包在应用内，完全不需要网络。

网页版可选的「把手牌存到电脑」功能，只会写入你自己选择的文件夹。

儿童

本应用仅为虚拟筹码的模拟对局，不含真钱。建议 13 岁以上使用（或遵循你所在地区对模拟赌博内容的最低年龄要求），我们不主动收集儿童数据。

购买

若通过应用商店付费购买，支付由商店方（Google / Microsoft）处理，我们只能看到聚合后的销售报表，拿不到你的支付信息或身份。
